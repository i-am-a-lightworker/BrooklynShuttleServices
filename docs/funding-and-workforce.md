# Funding & workforce reference

Verified during this project's research (checked live, Aug 2026 — reverify before
using specific figures in a funding application, since these programs shift).

## Currently live
- **NYC Community Justice Reentry Network (CJRN)** — Mayor's Office of Criminal
  Justice. Funds paid transitional employment for justice-involved NYC
  residents directly. Most direct city-level source for subsidizing
  reentry-hire wages.
- **DOL Reentry Employment Opportunities (REO) grants** — federal, via WIOA.
  Typically awarded to nonprofit intermediaries, not directly to a private
  employer. Practical move: partner with an existing REO grantee rather than
  applying directly.
- **DOJ Second Chance Act grants** — similar shape to REO, goes to
  agencies/nonprofits, recidivism-reduction focus.

## Not currently claimable — reverify before relying on it
- **Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC)** — lapsed Jan 1, 2026, pending
  Congressional reauthorization. Historically reinstated retroactively after
  past lapses (e.g. 2013–2014). Budget it as "captured when reauthorized," not
  as a current funding line. Was worth up to $2,400 per qualifying ex-felon
  hire in year one when active.
